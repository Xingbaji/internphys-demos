InternPhys书生·万殊One law, myriad forms

InternPhys is a GPU physics simulation engine from Shanghai AI Laboratory. Rigid bodies, soft bodies, cloth, rods, particles and robots share one energy and one double-precision implicit solver: one set of physical laws for every kind of object. Contact is computed as an integral over the two touching surfaces, so each contact counts exactly once and no two objects ever interpenetrate. Robots grasp through contact and friction alone, with no attachment shortcuts.

    Physically consistent contact (QUILT)

    Each contact region counts once, weighted by area. As a needle sweeps over mesh vertices, per-pair IPC inflates the contact force up to 35×; QUILT stays within 1%.

    Trustworthy friction and forces

    Objects that Coulomb friction should hold no longer creep out of the grip; reported contact forces satisfy momentum balance and can serve directly as tactile signals.

    Guaranteed intersection-free

    A barrier potential rules out interpenetration and element inversion at every step, with no per-scene contact stiffness tuning.

    One solver, many materials

    Rigid bodies, FEM / VEM soft bodies, cloth, rods, particles and enclosed fluid cavities are coupled in a single implicit time step, covering embodied-AI setups with robot arms, dexterous hands and visuotactile sensors.

    All videos are rendered from simulation results. “Solve” on a card is the solver time for the whole run on a single GPU (NVIDIA RTX 4090 / 5090), excluding rendering; “per frame” is the median solve time per frame.
